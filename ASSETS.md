# Artwork terms

The cast artwork in this repository (sprite frames under
`mac/frames/` and `mac/walk8-source/`, the face pack and
animated sequences under `faces/`, the source sheets, the
key art, and the motion loops) depicts real people, a real
cat, and the author's AI agents.

The artwork is published so the pipeline and the results
can be studied, not licensed for reuse. Do not redistribute
it, use it in another product, or train models on it. The
converters and slicers that produce artwork like it are
code, and are MIT licensed; point them at your own cast.
